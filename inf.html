Cyber Security Enforcement & Penalties

Official Notice: Liability & Legal Consequences for Unauthorized Access

Domain & Asset Hijacking Penalties

Subdomain Theft

50,000 PKR

Fine imposed per unauthorized sub-domain takeover or exploitation.

Root Domain Theft

100,000 PKR

Fine imposed for unauthorized control or transfer of primary domains.

TLD Exploitation

200,000 PKR

Penalty for unauthorized tampering with Top-Level Domain configurations.

Advanced Infrastructure Breach

400,000 PKR

Fine for high-level infrastructure manipulation and systemic breach.

Critical Network Takeover

1,000,000 PKR

Maximum penalty for full corporate network or enterprise-level theft.

Financial Fraud & Account Theft

Account Money Theft Liability

If a hacker steals funds or financial assets from any account, they are legally bound to pay a mandatory penalty of:

10x (Ten Times) the Stolen Amount

Formula: Total Penalty Liability = (Stolen Amount) × 10

Legal Enforcement

⚠️ Absolute Non-Compliance Warning

Failure to pay the designated fines or return the multiplied liabilities immediately will result in direct escalation. An official FIR (First Information Report) will be registered with the Cyber Crime Wing and local law enforcement agencies without further notice, initiating immediate criminal prosecution and arrest protocols.
